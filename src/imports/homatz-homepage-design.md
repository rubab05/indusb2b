Design a desktop-first B2B ecommerce homepage for HOMATZ, a household products supplier. The website should follow a category-led layout approach similar to SR Mailing, where categories are surfaced early, the browsing experience is clean and structured, and the site feels like a professional trade catalogue rather than a generic consumer store. The tone should be practical, polished, and product-led.

Brand context:
HOMATZ develops and sources high quality household products, specifically focused on everyday house items. HOMATZ is a multi-category supplier with products across:

Mats & Rugs

Decoration & Seasonal Products

Kitchen & Household

Garden & Outdoor

Toys & Games

Overall design direction:
Use a clean white and soft neutral base, charcoal typography, and restrained golden-yellow and muted-green accent colours inspired by the HOMATZ logo and product mix. The site should feel modern, spacious, and commercial. Avoid luxury styling, dark themes, or flashy consumer-market aesthetics. The layout should feel like a B2B wholesale website with strong category navigation and large product imagery.
